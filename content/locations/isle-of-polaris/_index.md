# Formation of Polaris

Long long ago, when the islands began splintering off from one another, it is widely accepted that the Isle of Polaris was split off from a land far greater in size than any of the islands currently floating through the skies. When Polaris was broken off, it was largely surrounded by a thick, unnatural smog, barely letting any light enter the island and shielding the inhabitants from the outside world. This smog encased the island for an exceptional amount of time, forcing the inhabitants to adapt to the abnormal phenomena.

Not much is known about the Polaris before the current tribe, the Locheyue, came to fruition. It was believed that the smog had a magical attribute to it, although not much else can be said about the time shortly after the Fracture, as historical texts were passed down by word of mouth before the Locheyue wrote it down, learning from some old texts in the temple on the island. 


# Geography

The Isle of Polaris is a decent size, with a couple of mountains towards the middle of the island. Most of the island is covered by trees of varying types, so when the smog cleared about 20 years before Marlowe’s birth, the sun did little to touch the soil of its lost land. 


# Flora

Most of the plants on the island were not reliant on the sun, so the trees attempted to grow as tall as they could, and grew thick branches with big leaves, creating a canopy so saturated the moon’s light would scarcely touch the ground. The few areas of grassland, and the mountains, were the only areas uncovered by the blanket of trees. 

Most other plants unfortunate enough to live beneath the canopy of trees had to adapt to the lack of light, and some flowers had evolved to give off a dim glow, aiding other growth on the forest floor. Depending on the hue of the glow, these flowers offer some medicinal effects, and the Locheyue quickly learned which flowers were best to use for different ailments and sicknesses, along with other plants naturally occurring on the island.
