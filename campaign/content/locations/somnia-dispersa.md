# Somnia Dispersa

*Somnia Dispersia, the realm of scattered dreams. Some believe it was created by a god who wished to combine every realm in existence into one plane, while others tell tales of a once-whole world fractured by selfish gods. Comparing the geography of Somnia Dispersia to an everything bagel would be fairly accurate, with a ring of thousands of unique islands orbiting a void referred to by sailors as The Pit. It leaves few survivors for those who venture close to it, but some claim to have survived the cyclone of wind and lightning that surround The Pit. All who sail know to take a curved path to avoid The Pit, lest they be consumed by the storm.*

*Almost as mysterious as The Pit is what happens to travelers that fall past the clouds below. The most accepted theory is that there is land deep, deep below, but not enough oxygen for most creatures to survive. Recent rumors have spread of a massive dragon that dwells in the depths, hoarding and consuming anything that falls past the clouds. Either way, it is ill-advised to explore these rumors unless you are prepared for the potential consequences.*

*The islands themselves vary in size, altitude, population, and intrigue! From the busy and bustling Feywild to the uninspired and underpopulated Vex, there's an island for every being. Some islands have a god bound to them, others are completely uninhabited. Most islands have their own set of rules and laws, but murder and theft is normally frowned upon under governed lands.*

*For those who don't wish to settle down, the skies offer freedom and adventure, if you're willing to work for it. There are no laws in the skies, so should someone cross you, you are in your right to expedite their death. The lack of rules incentivizes fools to do their wrongs, which gives wealthy individuals need for hunters to exact revenge on those who cross them. Individuals who trust Dolor Mitis to do whatever they require... for the right price.*
